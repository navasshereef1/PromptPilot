# Changelog

## 2.0.0

First public release.

- One-click Fix, Improve and Engineer inside any AI chat, with Undo
- Automatic detection of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and other AI sites
- 18 editable modes, including five for image prompts, plus your own
- Context system: About me, projects, current chat, clipboard, and questions before writing
- Six widget styles, with light and dark themes and accent colours
- Studio, template library, searchable history and insights
- Groq, Cerebras, Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral and Ollama, with automatic fallback
- Privacy shield for private details
- Event-driven design with about 0 idle CPU
